# NLI-gated hybrid generation smoke test

Version 2 failed on evidence use and citation generation. Version 3 is a different architecture: NLI predicts claim stance from evidence; the generator only summarizes a selected evidence passage. Do not describe this as a generative verdict model. Reuse both existing local models without downloading new weights.

Empty evidence abstains without generation. A passage supplies a candidate stance only when contradiction or entailment is its highest score, at least 0.6, with margin at least 0.15. Opposing qualified stances abstain. Choose the strongest qualified passage, breaking ties by input order. Generate a one-sentence summary from that passage. Accept it only if NLI entailment from the passage is at least 0.7 with margin at least 0.2 over contradiction and neutral. Otherwise abstain and preserve the raw rejected output. These fixed development thresholds are heuristics, not calibrated confidence or gold sufficiency.

Source IDs are attached by the application to the selected passage, not invented or generated by the model. This records provenance of the summary; it does not establish correct reasoning. NLI semantic checks can fail and share model biases. A summary of evidence is not necessarily an explanation connecting evidence to the claim. Source authentication, claim reasoning and full APV-RAG integration remain open.

Use the same four synthetic sanity cases, preserving both prior failures. Support and contradiction cases must have correct NLI stance and an accepted generated summary. Unrelated and empty evidence must have NEI stance, abstain, and avoid generation. The changed criterion follows the explicitly different hybrid contract; it does not retroactively pass version 2. These fixtures are software checks, not research annotations or publication results. Keep another full SciFact run blocked until the export is checked.
